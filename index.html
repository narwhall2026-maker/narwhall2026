Privacy & community rules

Last updated 25 October 2026. This is Narwhall’s current product notice; a full legal privacy notice and terms still need the owner’s review before a wider launch.

Where your photos go

Nothing uploads until you tap Pin. Main-wall photos are visible to you, site admins for moderation, and friends with an active peek. Locked-wall photos are visible to you, site admins for moderation, and friends you explicitly invite for 24 hours. Community photos are visible to signed-in members who have unlocked Community. Blocking can further restrict Community visibility.

Sharing and saving

A shared moment creates a separate copy on your friend’s main wall after they accept. It opens mutual main-wall peeks for 24 hours. Removing your own wall card does not remove that friend’s accepted copy. Locked-wall invitations can be revoked. People can save or screenshot visible photos; Narwhall does not prevent screenshots.

Camera, gallery and face effects

Your camera preview and narwhal face effect run on your device. Face frames and face positions are not sent to a face-tracking service or stored as face profiles. The tracking library and model download from jsDelivr and Google. Camera permission does not give admins access to watch your camera. Gallery access only includes photos you choose.

Account details and providers

Supabase handles sign-in and account/database records. Cloudflare R2 stores uploaded photos and a Worker serves authorised photo requests. Vercel hosts the app. Admins can see your email, username, photo counts, sign-in time, and approximate recent activity/device/browser details. Passwords are managed by Supabase Auth and are not visible to admins. Basic activity reporting can be turned off for this browser in Profile.

Be kind. Share with permission.

  • Post content you own or have permission to share. Respect consent, including when other people appear in a photo.
  • No harassment, threats, impersonation, exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, or sexual content involving minors.
  • Do not post someone’s private information, spam, or illegal content.
  • Report inappropriate Community posts using Report. You can block Community accounts in the app.

Moderation and deletion

Site admins can review main and locked walls for moderation. Community reports are available to the owner for review. You can hide your wall photos, manage invitations, block accounts, and request account deletion in Profile. Account deletion requires at least 30 days and admin review of the account and photos. The account stays usable while waiting, and requests can be cancelled until final deletion begins. An admin completes deletion after the wait and approval. Completed deletion removes uploaded photos and their in-app shared copies, but cannot erase copies someone saved outside Narwhall. Providers may retain backups for their normal retention periods.

Contact

Use Report on a Community post to flag a moderation concern. A dedicated privacy contact will be published when the owner finalises the launch policies.
